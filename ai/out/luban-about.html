About Us

Luban – Where Purity Becomes an Experience.

Founded in the heart of Oman in 1999, Luban Company was born from a deep passion to revive one of nature’s most extraordinary treasures: Omani Hojari Frankincense. This rare and sacred resin, carried on the winds of Dhofar for centuries, has long been a symbol of purity, healing, and timeless beauty — cherished by Egyptian queens to preserve youthful skin and trusted across generations for wellness and peace of mind.

For centuries, Frankincense has been revered as one of nature’s most powerful gifts, used across civilizations for its extraordinary healing and restorative properties. Scientific research today validates what ancient cultures instinctively knew — Frankincense is rich in potent compounds like boswellic acids that combat inflammation, promote cell regeneration, and strengthen the skin’s natural barrier. On the skin, it helps reduce fine lines, soothe irritation, and restore elasticity. For the body and hair, it deeply nourishes, calms dryness, and revitalizes from root to tip. In aromatherapy, the warm, resinous scent of Frankincense is clinically proven to reduce stress, enhance mood, and promote overall emotional balance.

At Luban, we don’t just create luxury products; we craft a holistic experience deeply rooted in Omani heritage and perfected with global standards. With our very own factory, we oversee every stage of production with meticulous care, ensuring that each product is made with the utmost ethics, transparency, and respect for nature.
We source only the purest, Grade 1 Hojari Frankincense, extracted using traditional methods from the Boswellia Sacra tree in the Dhofar mountains. Why Boswellia Sacra? Because it produces the highest concentration of therapeutic essential oils, unmatched clarity, and the richest aroma — qualities that no other Frankincense variety can rival. Why Dhofar? Its unique climate and fertile soil create ideal conditions for these ancient trees, making it the birthplace of the world’s finest Frankincense resin.

The oil we extract from these crystal-clear resins is the foundation of every Luban product — from skincare and body care to haircare and aromatherapy. Each formula is crafted with scientific precision and a deep respect for nature, ensuring powerful results that remain safe even for sensitive skin.

All our products are certified by the Ministry of Health and have received international awards for their purity and effectiveness. Today, we stand proudly as pioneers in offering the world’s finest Omani Hojari Frankincense Oil, blended into 100% natural and organic self-care rituals.

At Luban, we are more than a brand — we are a legacy of care, a bridge between Oman’s timeless traditions and modern luxury, where true beauty begins at the roots.
