Designer, Manufacturer & Integrator of Machines & Tooling Lomar Machine & Tool Company is a Designer, Manufacturer and Integrator of standard and custom machines for hose crimping, tube end forming, tube bending, brazing, leak test systems and assembly machines.

Additional products and services include rack and pinion gears, hydraulic systems and reservoirs, and job shop machining and fabrication services.

About Us

Lomar Machine & Tool Co. is a designer, manufacturer and integrator of standard and custom machines and tooling for component assembly, hose crimping, tube endforming, tube bending, brazing and leak testing.  Additional products and services include; hydraulic systems and reservoirs, industrial racks, pinions and gears, along with machining and fabrication services, 3D printing, prototype work, private label – contract manufacturing, technical support and repair services.

Markets served: Automotive, Aerospace, Agriculture, Appliance, Food, Furniture, HVAC & R, Medical and Military.

Capabilities

Engineering

Our in-house Engineering Department represents multiple disciplines of Mechanical, Electrical, Hydraulic, Pneumatic, and Tooling Design Engineers.

Manufacturing

Lomar manufacturing capabilities utilize the latest in CNC Turning, Milling and Grinding equipment as well as Wire and Plunge EDM.  Fabrication capabilities include CNC Laser cutting, CNC Metal Forming, Welding and Powder and Liquid coating.

Machine Build

All Machinery is built to meet NEC Standards. CE, CSA and NR12 Compliance are also available upon customer request. Electrical Wiring and PLC Programming is performed in-house.

Quality

Lomar Machine and Tool Company is ISO 9001: 2015 Certified. Components are inspected for final approval prior to shipping to both internal and external customers.

We approached Lomar to improve an existing tube forming process that was outdated and only producing 125 parts an hour.  They worked with our engineers and tool room to find out what had and had not work for us over the years and then came back with an fully automated machine that included a tube hopper, seam detection, length verification, transfer system and forming stations.
